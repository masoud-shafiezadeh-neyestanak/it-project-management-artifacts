## 1. Project Summary

### 1.1 Project Overview
Project Genesis is an operational readiness initiative designed to establish the digital infrastructure, standardized business processes, contractual templates, service portfolio, and project management workspace required to launch and manage an IT and business services enterprise in the Sultanate of Oman.

The project will use a hybrid delivery approach, combining structured governance and approval checkpoints with iterative implementation of technical and digital deliverables.

The primary objective is to establish a documented, secure, and operationally manageable business foundation without unnecessary upfront expenditure or premature expansion of permanent staffing.

### 1.2 Business Case
Establishing a sustainable operational presence in Oman requires coordinated digital infrastructure, documented operating procedures, consistent commercial documentation, and a professional digital presence.

Project Genesis will need to address these needs by:
- Establishing the company's domain, corporate email, cloud document repository, and baseline security controls.
- Developing reusable commercial and legal document templates.
- Publishing a bilingual corporate website and service catalogue.
- Implementing a centralized Asana workspace for operational coordination.
- Documenting standard operating procedures (SOPs) for relevant service and trade-related workflows.
- Initiating client acquisition through the preparation and dispatch of five formal proposals to qualified target SME clients.

The expected outcome is an operational foundation that supports service delivery, improves consistency, reduces administrative overhead, and provides a basis for controlled business growth.

## 4. Project Scope

### 4.1 In Scope
#### A. Digital and IT Infrastructure
- Corporate domain registration and DNS configuration.
- Corporate email setup.
- Cloud document repository configuration.
- Selection and configuration of an appropriate cloud hosting solution.
- Baseline identity and access management controls.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for supported administrative and business accounts.
- Backup and recovery arrangements appropriate to the selected services.
- Technical documentation and account ownership records.
- Basic security and functional verification.
*(Google Workspace may be used for corporate email, collaboration, and document management. AWS or another approved hosting provider may be used for website and application hosting, subject to the approved architecture and budget).*

#### B. Commercial and Legal Documentation
Preparation and review of the following five reusable templates:
1. Commercial Proposal Template.
2. Statement of Work (SOW).
3. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
4. Master Services Agreement (MSA).
5. Service Level Agreement (SLA).

#### C. Corporate Website and Service Portfolio
- Corporate website design and implementation (Arabic and English).
- Publication of the approved service catalogue.
- Company contact information and business inquiry mechanism.
- Basic responsive design, browser compatibility checks, and HTTPS configuration.
- LinkedIn company page creation or standardization.

#### D. Operational Management Workspace
- Asana workspace configuration, project/task organization, and assignment of responsibilities.
- Custom fields, workflow definitions, operational documentation, and task templates.
- Initial backlog, implementation tracking, and user acceptance checks.

#### E. Trade and Import/Export Procedures
- Documentation of relevant internal workflows associated with activity code 461003.
- Preparation of applicable SOPs, process checklists, and identification of required approvals.

#### F. Client Acquisition Preparation
- Identification of suitable target SME clients and preparation of five formal commercial proposals.
- Dispatch of proposals and recording of dispatch dates, recipients, and follow-up status.

#### G. Project Closure
- Deliverable acceptance review, infrastructure/documentation handover, and Closeout Report.

### 4.2 Out of Scope
- Construction of residential or non-residential buildings (including Code 410001).
- Physical real estate acquisition.
- Permanent full-time employee recruitment during the project.
- Development of unrelated software products or large-scale enterprise applications.
- Implementation of a production payment gateway unless specifically approved.
- Guaranteed client conversion, signed commercial contracts, revenue, or market share.
- Ongoing managed services and long-term maintenance beyond agreed handover.

### 4.3 Scope Change Control
Any proposed change affecting deliverables, timeline, cost, quality, security, legal compliance, or resource requirements must be documented in a Change Request and submitted to the Project Sponsor for approval.
