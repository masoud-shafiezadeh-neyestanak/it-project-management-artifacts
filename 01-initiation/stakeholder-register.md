## 2. RACI Matrix (Responsibility Assignment)

* **R (Responsible):** The role that performs the activity.
* **A (Accountable):** The single role with final approval and ownership (Strictly ONE 'A' per task).
* **C (Consulted):** Role providing advice or vital information.
* **I (Informed):** Role notified of progress and outcomes.
