## 1. Project Summary

### 1.1 Project Overview

Project Genesis is a real internal business and operational readiness initiative being undertaken within an Oman-registered company with an active Commercial Registration (CR).

The project focuses on establishing and strengthening the company's digital infrastructure, corporate web presence, commercial documentation, operational management environment, service portfolio, and initial client outreach capabilities.

The initiative is intended to support the company's actual business activities by creating a structured, secure, and operationally manageable foundation for service delivery and business development.

Project Genesis will use a Hybrid Project Management approach, combining structured governance, documented responsibilities, approval checkpoints, and baseline controls with iterative implementation and testing where appropriate.

The project is managed by the appointed Project Manager, who is responsible for coordinating planning, execution, monitoring, stakeholder engagement, risk management, and project documentation within the authority delegated by the Project Sponsor.

### 1.2 Business Case

A sustainable and professionally managed business operation requires more than company registration. It also requires appropriate digital infrastructure, reliable administrative systems, documented operating procedures, standardized commercial documentation, and a credible corporate presence.

Although the company has an active Commercial Registration, its operational capabilities and supporting business infrastructure require coordinated development.

Project Genesis addresses these needs through the following activities:

- Establishing corporate domain, email, collaboration, and cloud infrastructure.
- Implementing baseline information security and access-management controls.
- Developing a bilingual corporate website and digital service catalogue.
- Preparing reusable commercial and legal document templates.
- Establishing a centralized Asana workspace for project coordination and operational workflows.
- Developing relevant Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and operational checklists.
- Preparing and dispatching formal commercial proposals to qualified SME prospects.
- Establishing documented project governance, monitoring, asset handover, and closure arrangements.

The expected business outcome is a more organized and operationally capable company, supported by documented processes, appropriately controlled digital assets, and a foundation for sustainable business development.

## 4. Project Scope

### 4.1 In-Scope Activities

The project comprises seven principal deliverables.

#### D1 — Digital Infrastructure

Establish the company's foundational digital infrastructure, including:

- Corporate domain registration and DNS configuration.
- Corporate email and collaboration workspace setup.
- Cloud document repository configuration.
- Selection and configuration of an appropriate hosting or cloud solution.
- Identity and access management controls.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for supported administrative and business accounts.
- Least-privilege access arrangements.
- Appropriate backup and recovery arrangements.
- Technical documentation and administrative ownership records.
- Baseline security and functional verification.

Google Workspace may be used for corporate email, collaboration, and document management. AWS or another approved provider may be used for hosting and related cloud requirements, subject to the approved architecture, security requirements, and budget.

#### D2 — Corporate Web Presence

Design, implement, test, and publish a responsive bilingual corporate website in Arabic and English.

The website will include:

- Approved corporate information.
- Verified corporate service descriptions.
- Appropriate contact and business inquiry mechanisms.
- Responsive design for agreed desktop and mobile configurations.
- HTTPS configuration.
- Basic browser compatibility checks.
- Functional and baseline security testing.
- Business-owner review and approval of published content.

The website must accurately represent the company's verified service portfolio.

Search-engine ranking, website traffic, customer conversion, and revenue are not guaranteed project outcomes.

#### D3 — Commercial and Legal Template Suite

Prepare five reusable commercial and legal document templates:

1. Commercial Proposal Template.
2. Statement of Work (SOW).
3. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
4. Master Services Agreement (MSA).
5. Service Level Agreement (SLA).

The templates must be reviewed by relevant business stakeholders and, where appropriate, a qualified legal adviser familiar with applicable Omani requirements.

Review comments, unresolved legal questions, and required revisions must be documented before the templates are authorized for organizational use.

Approval of standard templates does not eliminate the need for transaction-specific review when entering into future commercial agreements.

#### D4 — Operational Workspace

Establish a centralized Asana workspace to support project coordination and operational workflows.

The workspace will include:

- Agreed project and task hierarchy.
- Defined responsibilities and workflow ownership.
- Task templates and appropriate custom fields.
- Milestone and progress tracking.
- Iterative delivery boards or backlogs where suitable.
- Operational and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) checklists.
- Appropriate account and workspace access controls.
- Coordination with GitHub and the organization's documentation practices.
- Repository and secret-management controls.

Scrum-specific roles, ceremonies, and practices will be used only where they are explicitly adopted for relevant work.

#### D5 — Digital Service Catalogue

Prepare and publish a digital service catalogue describing the company's verified and approved service portfolio.

The catalogue will:

- Describe relevant services and intended SME client needs.
- Use descriptions verified against the company's current CR.
- Include appropriate business contact channels.
- Identify relevant service scope and exclusions.
- Include prices or service-level commitments only after the necessary business and legal reviews.
- Avoid claims that imply authorization for unverified or unlicensed activities.

The catalogue may be published on the corporate website or another approved digital channel.

It must not be published as a definitive representation of authorized company activities until the relevant registration and service information has been verified.

#### D6 — Client Outreach Package

Prepare and dispatch five formal commercial proposals to qualified SME prospects in the agreed Muscat and South Al Batinah target areas.

The work includes:

- Identifying and qualifying appropriate SME prospects.
- Preparing customized proposals using the approved commercial proposal template.
- Aligning proposals with verified services and authorized commercial terms.
- Recording proposal references and dispatch dates.
- Maintaining recipient and follow-up records.
- Retaining appropriate dispatch evidence in a controlled project repository.

The objective is to complete and document five proposal dispatches. The project does not guarantee client responses, signed agreements, revenue, or commercial conversion.

#### D7 — Project Closeout Package

Complete the formal project closeout process, including:

- Review of all in-scope deliverables.
- Verification of completion and acceptance criteria.
- Technical and operational asset handover.
- Documentation of outstanding issues and exceptions.
- Recording of follow-up actions and accountable owners.
- Final budget summary.
- Lessons Learned Retrospective.
- Preparation of the Project Closeout Report.
- Formal closure approval by the Project Sponsor or authorized delegate.

Any unfinished deliverable or approved exception must be documented with its status, accountable owner, and agreed follow-up action.

### 4.2 Out-of-Scope Activities

The following activities are excluded unless formally introduced through approved scope change control:

- Construction of residential or non-residential buildings.
- Physical real estate acquisition or major facilities development.
- Permanent full-time employee recruitment during the planned project execution window.
- Development of unrelated software products or large-scale enterprise applications beyond the approved corporate website scope.
- Production payment gateway implementation.
- Execution of regulated or otherwise unauthorized commercial activities.
- Import/export transactions beyond the approved internal documentation and procedure-development scope.
- Guaranteed client conversion, signed contracts, revenue, or market share.
- Long-term managed IT services and continuous maintenance beyond the agreed handover arrangements.
- Additional deliverables not included in the approved Scope Baseline.

### 4.3 Scope Change Control

Any proposed change affecting project deliverables, schedule, budget, quality, security, regulatory exposure, or resource requirements must be documented through a Change Request.

The Project Manager will assess the impact and provide a recommendation to the Project Sponsor.

Changes to approved project baselines require authorization by the Project Sponsor or an appropriately delegated company representative.

No material scope expansion may be treated as approved merely because it has been discussed informally.

Project Genesis will operate through a defined governance structure intended to clarify accountability, decision-making, and approval responsibilities.

The Project Sponsor provides organizational direction and is responsible for decisions beyond the delegated authority of the Project Manager.

Sponsor responsibilities include:

- Formal approval of the Project Charter.
- Approval of the project scope, schedule, and cost baselines.
- Authorization of material scope or budget changes.
- Decisions concerning material risks and resource constraints.
- Acceptance of major deliverables where designated.
- Formal approval of project closure.

**Project Manager**

The Project Manager is responsible for day-to-day project management and coordination within the approved authority.

Responsibilities include:

- Maintaining the Project Charter and supporting project documentation.
- Coordinating project planning and delivery.
- Maintaining the scope, schedule, and budget records.
- Managing stakeholder communication and engagement.
- Maintaining risk, issue, decision, and change records.

The authorized company representative is responsible for approving or signing commercial commitments, corporate representations, and other matters within the person's formally delegated organizational authority.
