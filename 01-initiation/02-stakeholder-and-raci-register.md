## 1. Purpose and Objectives

### 1.1 Purpose

This document identifies the key internal and external stakeholders of Project Genesis, evaluates their influence and interest, defines their expectations, and establishes appropriate engagement strategies.

It also provides a Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI) to clarify accountability, execution responsibilities, consultation requirements, and information-sharing obligations across the project's work packages.

The Stakeholder Register and RACI Matrix establish a consistent basis for stakeholder coordination, decision-making, communication management, and project governance.

### 1.2 Objectives

The objectives of this document are to:

- Identify individuals, groups, organizations, and external parties that may influence or be affected by the project.
- Assess stakeholder power, interest, expectations, and engagement requirements.
- Define appropriate communication and stakeholder engagement strategies.
- Establish clear responsibility and accountability for project activities.
- Clarify decision-making and approval authority.
- Maintain alignment between the Project Charter, Scope Baseline, Risk Register, Communication Plan, and WBS.
- Reduce ambiguity, duplicated responsibilities, and approval delays.

### 1.3 Stakeholder Management Principles

Project Genesis will apply the following principles:

- Every material project stakeholder must be identified and assigned an appropriate engagement approach.
- Stakeholder expectations must be understood and managed throughout the project lifecycle.
- Every project work package must have one designated Accountable owner.
- Responsibility assignments must remain consistent with the approved WBS and Scope Baseline.
- External advisers provide specialist advice within their engagement but do not automatically hold internal project approval authority.
- Regulatory authorities and prospective clients are treated as external stakeholders, not as members of the internal project delivery team.
- Stakeholder information and engagement strategies must be reviewed when project conditions or stakeholder relationships change.

## 2. Stakeholder Identification & Register

The following register identifies the principal stakeholders and stakeholder groups relevant to Project Genesis.

Power and interest assessments are qualitative and represent the anticipated influence and involvement of each stakeholder in this project. They must be reviewed against actual organizational arrangements during project mobilization.

**Stakeholder Identification Notes:**

- The Project Sponsor should be a formally designated individual with sufficient authority to approve project baselines and material decisions. The Executive Board may provide overall governance, but the designated Sponsor should serve as the primary approval contact.
- The Authorized Company Signatory may be the same individual as the Project Sponsor. If so, the two functions should be documented as separate decision responsibilities rather than treated as two different people.
- The Data Protection Officer or designated data protection contact must be identified according to applicable legal and organizational requirements. This role must not be assumed to be filled until formally designated.
- The Database Administrator is a conditional resource and should only be treated as an active project stakeholder if the approved technical architecture requires the role.
- Target SME prospects are potential customers. Their actual interest must be assessed as part of the qualification and outreach process.
- Regulatory authorities are external stakeholders whose decisions and requirements may affect project activities. They do not form part of the internal project delivery team.
