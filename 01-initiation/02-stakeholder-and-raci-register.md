## 1. Purpose and Objectives

### 1.1 Purpose

This document identifies the key internal and external stakeholders of Project Genesis, evaluates their influence and interest, defines their expectations, and establishes appropriate engagement strategies.

It also provides a Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI) to clarify accountability, execution responsibilities, consultation requirements, and information-sharing obligations across the project's work packages.

The Stakeholder Register and RACI Matrix establish a consistent basis for stakeholder coordination, decision-making, communication management, and project governance.

### 1.2 Objectives

The objectives of this document are to:

- Identify individuals, groups, organizations, and external parties that may influence or be affected by the project.
- Assess stakeholder power, interest, expectations, and engagement requirements.
- Define appropriate communication and stakeholder engagement strategies.
- Establish clear responsibility and accountability for project activities.
- Clarify decision-making and approval authority.
- Maintain alignment between the Project Charter, Scope Baseline, Risk Register, Communication Plan, and WBS.
- Reduce ambiguity, duplicated responsibilities, and approval delays.

### 1.3 Stakeholder Management Principles

Project Genesis will apply the following principles:

- Every material project stakeholder must be identified and assigned an appropriate engagement approach.
- Stakeholder expectations must be understood and managed throughout the project lifecycle.
- Every project work package must have one designated Accountable owner.
- Responsibility assignments must remain consistent with the approved WBS and Scope Baseline.
- External advisers provide specialist advice within their engagement but do not automatically hold internal project approval authority.
- Regulatory authorities and prospective clients are treated as external stakeholders, not as members of the internal project delivery team.
- Stakeholder information and engagement strategies must be reviewed when project conditions or stakeholder relationships change.

**Stakeholder Identification Notes:**

- The Project Sponsor should be a formally designated individual with sufficient authority to approve project baselines and material decisions. The Executive Board may provide overall governance, but the designated Sponsor should serve as the primary approval contact.
- The Authorized Company Signatory may be the same individual as the Project Sponsor. If so, the two functions should be documented as separate decision responsibilities rather than treated as two different people.
- The Data Protection Officer or designated data protection contact must be identified according to applicable legal and organizational requirements. This role must not be assumed to be filled until formally designated.
- The Database Administrator is a conditional resource and should only be treated as an active project stakeholder if the approved technical architecture requires the role.
- Target SME prospects are potential customers. Their actual interest must be assessed as part of the qualification and outreach process.
- Regulatory authorities are external stakeholders whose decisions and requirements may affect project activities. They do not form part of the internal project delivery team.

### 2.2 Power-Interest Analysis

Power and interest are assessed as follows:

**Power:** The stakeholder's ability to influence project decisions, authorize resources, approve changes, affect regulatory compliance, or constrain project delivery.

**Interest:** The stakeholder's level of involvement, concern, or direct interest in the project's objectives and outcomes.

The following engagement strategies are applied:

| Power | Interest | Recommended Strategy | Management Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | High | Manage Closely | Maintain active involvement, regular communication, and direct engagement in material decisions. |
| High | Low | Keep Satisfied | Provide relevant updates, meet formal requirements, and avoid unnecessary communication. |
| Low | High | Keep Informed | Maintain regular information sharing and seek feedback when appropriate. |
| Low | Low | Monitor | Monitor changes in interest or influence and engage when necessary. |

For stakeholders with Medium power or variable interest, the Lead Project Manager will tailor engagement based on their actual influence, responsibilities, and involvement.

The Power-Interest Grid is a planning and prioritization tool. It does not replace statutory communication obligations, contractual responsibilities, or formal approval requirements.

## 3. Stakeholder Expectations & Engagement Strategy

### 3.1 Project Sponsor / Executive Board

**Key Expectations:**

- Delivery within the approved scope, schedule, and cost baseline.
- Transparent reporting of material risks and project performance.
- Timely escalation of issues requiring executive decisions.
- Evidence-based acceptance of major deliverables.
- Controlled management of changes and external commitments.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Weekly executive status reporting.
- Formal milestone reviews.
- Escalation of high risks and material baseline deviations.
- Documented approval of scope, budget, schedule changes, and project closure.

### 3.2 Lead Project Manager

**Key Expectations:**

- Clear delegation of authority.
- Timely stakeholder responses and approvals.
- Access to required project resources.
- Accurate technical, commercial, and financial information.
- Consistent reporting and document ownership.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Continuous operational coordination.
- Regular reviews of the WBS, Risk Register, and Communication Plan.
- Maintenance of decision, issue, and change records.
- Coordination of project meetings and stakeholder engagement activities.

### 3.3 Technical Contributors

**Key Expectations:**

- Clear technical requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Approved infrastructure architecture.
- Appropriate access and technical resources.
- Timely resolution of dependencies and blockers.
- Clear source-code, configuration, and asset ownership arrangements.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Technical coordination through Asana and GitHub.
- Technical reviews at relevant implementation milestones.
- Documented pull-request reviews and configuration changes.
- Secure access and formal technical handover.

### 3.4 Marketing Lead

**Key Expectations:**

- Approved service portfolio and business information.
- Clear brand and communication requirements.
- Timely feedback on Arabic and English content.
- Commercially accurate service descriptions and pricing information.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Weekly content and service catalogue reviews.
- Coordination with the Lead Project Manager.
- Review and approval of external-facing materials before publication.
- Tracking of prospect qualification and proposal dispatch activities.

### 3.5 External Legal Adviser

**Key Expectations:**

- Accurate corporate and commercial information.
- Clearly defined review scope and requested deliverables.
- Timely access to relevant documents.
- Sufficient time to identify and communicate legal concerns.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Engage during preparation and review of contractual templates.
- Provide relevant legal and regulatory information through approved channels.
- Document comments and required corrective actions.
- Escalate issues that require additional legal analysis.

The External Legal Adviser provides specialist legal advice and review. Final business authorization and execution of company contracts remain with the appropriately authorized company representative.

### 3.6 Authorized Company Signatory / Business Owner

**Key Expectations:**

- Accurate representation of approved business activities.
- Control over commercial commitments and contractual terms.
- Approval of external-facing business information.
- Appropriate authorization of proposals and agreements.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Consult during service catalogue preparation and commercial template review.
- Obtain required authorization before formal commercial commitments.
- Involve the signatory in material client-facing decisions.
- Maintain documented approval records.

### 3.7 Data Protection Contact / DPO

**Key Expectations:**

- Appropriate handling of customer and prospect personal data.
- Clear understanding of data collection and processing purposes.
- Appropriate access and retention arrangements.
- Timely escalation of privacy concerns and suspected data incidents.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Consult during website form and customer-data design.
- Review relevant personal data handling and outreach procedures.
- Participate in assessment of material privacy incidents where applicable.
- Coordinate with legal advisers and the Project Sponsor when required.

### 3.8 Regulatory Authorities

**Key Expectations:**

- Accurate registration and business information.
- Compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.
- Timely submission of required documentation.
- Appropriate use of registered and authorized commercial activities.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Communicate through official channels where required.
- Verify commercial registration and activity information.
- Complete applicable administrative and regulatory requirements.
- Maintain relevant submission and approval records.

Regulatory interactions must be coordinated by the authorized company representative or designated project owner.

### 3.9 Cloud, Domain, and SaaS Providers

**Key Expectations:**

- Accurate account and service information.
- Compliance with contractual service terms.
- Timely payment and subscription management.
- Appropriate technical configuration and account administration.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Manage service activation and technical dependencies.
- Maintain records of subscriptions, service configurations, and administrative ownership.
- Escalate service issues through provider support channels.
- Review vendor suitability and recurring costs.

### 3.10 Target SME Prospects and Potential Clients

**Key Expectations:**

- Professional and understandable proposals.
- Transparent scope, assumptions, exclusions, and pricing.
- Clear service-level commitments.
- Appropriate confidentiality and information-handling practices.
- Timely responses to business inquiries.

**Engagement Strategy:**

- Qualify prospects according to business relevance.
- Use approved commercial proposal templates.
- Communicate through authorized corporate channels.
- Maintain a controlled outreach register.
- Track responses and feedback without assuming guaranteed commercial conversion.
