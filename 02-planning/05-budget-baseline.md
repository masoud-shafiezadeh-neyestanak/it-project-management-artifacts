## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the preliminary budget framework for Project Genesis.

It identifies the main cost categories, defines the cost estimation and monitoring approach, records the current preliminary project cost estimate, and describes the approval and change-control requirements.

The budget will become an approved cost baseline only after detailed estimates have been reviewed and formally authorized by the Project Sponsor.

## 3. Budget Scope

The project budget covers costs directly associated with the approved Project Genesis scope and its seven principal deliverables:

- D1 — Digital Infrastructure
- D2 — Corporate Web Presence
- D3 — Commercial & Legal Template Suite
- D4 — Operational Workspace
- D5 — Digital Service Catalogue
- D6 — Client Outreach Package
- D7 — Project Closeout Package

Costs arising from out-of-scope activities, including unapproved integrations, payment gateway implementation, additional infrastructure, or major scope changes, are excluded unless authorized through the formal change-control process.
