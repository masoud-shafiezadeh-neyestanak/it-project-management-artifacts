## 1. Executive Summary and Communication Objectives

### 1.1 Purpose

This Project Communication Management Plan defines the communication framework, stakeholder information requirements, communication channels, reporting arrangements, meeting practices, documentation standards, and escalation procedures necessary to support Project Genesis.

Project Genesis is a real business and operational readiness initiative undertaken within Bridge of Tomorrow, an Oman-registered company with an active Commercial Registration (CR).

The purpose of this plan is to ensure that relevant information reaches the appropriate stakeholders through suitable channels, at the right time, and in a format that supports effective coordination, informed decisions, controlled project delivery, and business operations.

The plan covers communication across:

- Project governance and authorization.
- Project planning and monitoring.
- Technical implementation and cloud infrastructure.
- Operational workflow development.
- Commercial and legal review.
- Data protection and information security.
- Service catalogue development.
- Client outreach and business development.
- Deliverable acceptance and project closure.

### 1.2 Communication Objectives

The communication management approach aims to:

- Maintain transparency regarding project progress, deliverables, schedule, budget, and material dependencies.
- Establish clear communication between the Project Manager, Project Sponsor, authorized company representatives, and assigned project contributors.
- Provide concise and decision-oriented management reporting.
- Support coordination through Asana, GitHub, Google Workspace, and other approved channels.
- Maintain traceable records of decisions, approvals, issues, change requests, and action items.
- Ensure material risks, blockers, and deviations are escalated to the appropriate decision-makers.
- Protect confidential business information, personal data, and security-sensitive information.
- Support appropriate communication with external advisers, service providers, regulatory authorities, and SME prospects.
- Distinguish informal coordination and verbal endorsement from formally recorded approvals.
- Maintain consistent communication responsibilities across the project lifecycle.

### 1.3 Communication Principles

**1. Audience-Appropriate Communication**

Information must be tailored to the recipient's responsibilities, decision-making authority, and information requirements.

Management communications should focus on project progress, material risks, deviations, business impact, and required decisions.

Technical communications should provide sufficient detail to support implementation, troubleshooting, security review, and verification.

Commercial and legal communications must use approved business information and appropriately restricted channels.

**2. TL;DR Reporting Principle**

Executive status reports, significant escalation messages, and decision requests should begin with a concise summary identifying:

- Overall situation or project status.
- Key issue, risk, or decision required.
- Relevant deadline or requested action.

Supporting details and evidence should follow where necessary.

**3. RAG Status Reporting**

Red, Amber, and Green indicators will be used consistently in project status reports and milestone reviews.

| Status | Definition | Management Response |
|---|---|---|
| Green — On Track | Work is progressing according to the applicable approved baseline, or, before formal baseline approval, according to the latest reviewed planning assumptions, with no unresolved critical concern. | Continue planned activities and routine monitoring. Clearly identify any planning assumptions that have not yet been approved. |
| Amber — At Risk | A potential deviation, dependency, resource constraint, or material issue exists but can reasonably be managed within delegated authority. | Assign corrective actions, monitor closely, and report to the Project Manager. |
| Red — Critical | An approved baseline is forecast to be breached, a High risk or serious incident requires intervention, or a decision beyond delegated authority is necessary. | Escalate promptly to the Project Sponsor or authorized company representative and initiate an appropriate response. |

**Pre-Baseline Reporting Rule:**

Until the relevant project baselines have been formally approved, RAG assessments must not imply that an approved schedule or cost baseline exists.

The Project Manager must identify when progress is being assessed against planning assumptions rather than authorized baselines.

**4. 5W1H Communication Alignment**

Each planned communication event should identify:

- **Who:** Intended audience and responsible owner.
- **What:** Information, update, or decision being communicated.
- **When:** Frequency, timing, or triggering event.
- **Where:** Approved communication channel.
- **Why:** Purpose and expected outcome.
- **How:** Format, supporting evidence, and record location.

**5. Transparency and Traceability**

Material decisions, changes, approvals, commitments, and exceptions must be documented in the appropriate project record.

Informal messages or verbal discussions do not replace required formal approvals.

The Project Sponsor's verbal endorsement of Project Genesis must be distinguished from formal approval of this plan, the Project Charter, or the project baselines.

**6. Information Security**

Confidential information, personal data, credentials, and security-sensitive material must be handled through approved channels and according to organizational access and information-handling requirements.

Public repository content must be limited to material approved for public disclosure.

### 3.2 Channel Usage Guidelines

**1. GitHub Repository**

GitHub serves as the version-controlled repository for:

- Project management documents approved for public disclosure.
- Non-sensitive technical documentation.
- Architecture descriptions and sanitized implementation records.
- Technical SOPs and configuration documentation.
- Approved Markdown project artifacts.

Public repositories must not contain:

- Confidential company information.
- Client personal data.
- Private contractual documents.
- Sensitive Commercial Registration details.
- Credentials, tokens, API keys, or other secrets.
- Restricted financial or legal records.
- Security incident details.

GitHub is not the authoritative repository for signed legal originals or confidential commercial records unless an appropriately controlled private environment has been explicitly approved.

Public project documentation must be reviewed before publication to confirm that business-sensitive information has been removed or appropriately sanitized.

The organization-controlled Asana workspace serves as the operational coordination platform.

- Task management and assignment.

- Official corporate emails.
- Management reporting.
- Legal template reviews.

Google Meet may be used for:

- Stakeholder meetings.
- Project status discussions.
- Technical and operational reviews.
- Deliverable demonstrations.
- Lessons learned sessions.
- Legal and commercial review meetings where appropriate.

**5. Approved Secure Messaging**

Approved messaging channels may be used for rapid operational coordination during agreed business hours.

They may support:

- Time-sensitive operational coordination.
- Short progress updates.
- Immediate notification of blockers.
- Meeting coordination.
- Urgent communication requiring prompt attention.

Messaging channels must not be treated as the authoritative record for formal approvals, contractual commitments, or sensitive information.

Material decisions made through messaging must be formally documented and linked to the relevant project record, normally within one business day.

## 4. Meeting Management and Documentation

### 4.1 Meeting Preparation

Meetings should be organized according to their purpose and should involve only stakeholders required to contribute, review, or make decisions.

Where appropriate:

- Agendas should be shared before scheduled meetings.
- Participants should receive relevant documents in advance.
- Required decisions and expected outcomes should be identified.
- Meeting duration should be proportionate to the subject and complexity.
- Confidential materials should be distributed through approved restricted channels.
- Meetings requiring formal decisions should identify the authorized decision-maker.
- Daily coordination should remain asynchronous by default, with synchronous meetings arranged when necessary.

### 4.2 Meeting Types

| Meeting Type | Purpose | Participants | Proposed Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Coordination | Review tasks, dependencies, blockers, and immediate priorities | Project Manager and assigned contributors | Weekdays, asynchronous by default |
| Weekly Project Review | Review project progress, RAG status, risks, issues, and decisions required | Project Manager and Project Sponsor or authorized representative, once designated | Weekly |
| Technical Review | Review technical configuration, architecture, security, and implementation progress | Project Manager and assigned technical contributor | During active technical delivery and major checkpoints |
| Legal and Commercial Review | Review contractual templates, service descriptions, and relevant legal requirements | Project Manager, External Legal Adviser when engaged, and Authorized Company Representative as required | At relevant review milestones |
| Deliverable Review | Demonstrate and assess deliverable readiness against acceptance criteria | Project Manager, assigned contributors, and authorized approver | At relevant milestone or iteration completion |
| Project Closure Review | Review handover, open items, lessons learned, and final acceptance | Project Manager, Sponsor or authorized representative, and relevant asset owners | During project closure |

### 4.3 Meeting Minutes and Action Tracking

The meeting owner or designated note-taker must document material discussions and decisions.

Meeting records should contain:

- Meeting title and date.
- Participants and relevant roles.
- Key discussion points.
- Decisions and approval outcomes.
- Identified issues and dependencies.
- Assigned action items.
- Action owners and target completion dates.
- Relevant document or WBS references.

Meeting notes and material action records should normally be published within one business day.

Meeting records containing confidential or personal information must be stored in a restricted location.

### 4.4 Decision Documentation

Material decisions must be recorded in the Project Decision Log or the appropriate formal approval record.

Each decision should include:

- Decision reference.
- Date and decision authority.
- Description and rationale.
- Affected scope, schedule, budget, or deliverable.
- Alternatives considered where relevant.
- Required follow-up action.
- Relevant document or approval reference.

A decision communicated informally must not be treated as formal authorization until the required approval is recorded.

### 4.5 Action Item Management

Actions identified during meetings, reviews, and escalations must be:

- Clearly described.
- Assigned to one accountable owner.
- Given an agreed target date.
- Recorded in Asana or the appropriate controlled action register.
- Reviewed during the next relevant coordination meeting.
- Escalated when overdue actions threaten project delivery or compliance.

## 5. Escalation Framework and Decision Authority

The escalation framework establishes how operational issues, schedule deviations, budget concerns, project risks, security incidents, and material decisions move through the project governance structure.

### 5.1 Escalation Levels

**Level 1 — Operational / Assigned Task Owner**

Responsible roles: Assigned Task Owners, Technical Contributors, Marketing Contributors, and other formally engaged project contributors.

Decision authority:

- Day-to-day execution of assigned tasks.
- Routine technical and operational adjustments within approved requirements.
- Resolution of minor blockers within assigned work-package boundaries.
- Internal coordination and corrective actions.
- Reporting of progress and identified issues.

Escalation triggers:

- A task blocker remains unresolved for more than one business day.
- A technical or operational dependency prevents expected progress.
- A task is forecast to miss its agreed target.
- An issue requires coordination beyond the task owner's authority.
- A security, privacy, or regulatory concern is identified.

The Task Owner must document the issue and notify the Project Manager when escalation is required.

Where a work package has no formally assigned contributor, the Project Manager retains coordination responsibility and must arrange the required resource before the work proceeds.

**Level 2 — Project Management / Project Manager**

Responsible authority: Project Manager — Masoud Shafiezadeh Neyestanak.

Decision authority:

- Task resequencing within the applicable approved baseline or agreed planning arrangement.
- Coordination of resources within delegated authority.
- Monitoring and coordination of risk mitigation actions.
- Vendor follow-up and operational coordination.
- Review of schedule and cost forecasts.
- Recommendation of corrective actions and change requests.
- Coordination of issue resolution and stakeholder communication.
- Preparation of escalation briefs and management recommendations.

Escalation triggers to Level 3:

- A forecast schedule variance exceeds two business days or threatens an approved milestone.
- Forecast expenditure exceeds the approved budget or delegated spending authority.
- A material scope change is required.
- A risk receives a High rating (score of 15 or higher).
- A significant legal, regulatory, privacy, or security concern is identified.
- A decision exceeds the Project Manager's delegated authority.
- A material resource constraint threatens an agreed deliverable.

Until a Schedule or Cost Baseline is formally approved, schedule and cost concerns must be assessed against the latest reviewed planning assumptions and existing company spending authority.

**Level 3 — Project Governance / Project Sponsor or Authorized Company Representative**

Responsible authority: Project Sponsor or formally authorized company representative, following confirmation of the applicable decision authority.

Decision authority, subject to formal delegation:

- Approval of material changes to project scope and objectives.
- Approval of changes to an authorized budget baseline.
- Approval of material schedule baseline revisions.
- Resolution of escalated stakeholder conflicts.
- Decisions concerning material residual risks.
- Decisions concerning project suspension or termination.
- Authorization of material commercial commitments within delegated company authority.
- Authorization of formal external communications where required.
- Approval of final project closure.

Escalation triggers:

- A forecast schedule delay exceeds five business days.
- An approved budget baseline is forecast to be exceeded.
- A High risk remains without an approved response or requires formal risk acceptance.
- A material legal, regulatory, privacy, or cybersecurity concern arises.
- A formal scope or baseline change requires Sponsor approval.
- The Project Manager requires a decision or resource commitment beyond delegated authority.

Serious security incidents, suspected personal data breaches, potential data protection violations, and material regulatory concerns must be escalated immediately, irrespective of routine schedule or financial thresholds.

Until formal designation is completed, matters requiring Sponsor-level decisions must be referred to the currently authorized company representative under the company's existing authority arrangements.

### 5.2 RAG and Escalation Alignment

| Condition | Reporting Status | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| Progress within approved baselines; no unresolved critical concern | Green | Routine monitoring and reporting. |
| Progress consistent with current planning assumptions before baseline approval | Green — Planning | Report progress and explicitly identify unapproved assumptions. |
| Forecast deviation manageable within delegated authority | Amber | Project Manager assesses and assigns corrective actions. |
| High risk (score ≥ 15) | Red | Prompt escalation to the authorized company representative. |
| Forecast breach of approved budget or material baseline | Red | Appropriate governance review and formal decision. |
| Material legal, regulatory, privacy, or security concern | Red | Immediate escalation and appropriate response. |
| Critical milestone threatens approved project completion | Red | Governance intervention and recovery or change decision. |

Reporting status must reflect actual forecast exposure.

A low numerical risk score does not prevent escalation of a serious legal, security, or operational issue.

### 5.3 Escalation Procedure

**Step 1 — Identification**

The assigned Task Owner or relevant stakeholder identifies the issue, records available facts, and logs the matter in Asana or the appropriate restricted issue register.

For security or privacy incidents, the approved incident reporting procedure must be followed immediately.

**Step 2 — Initial Assessment**

The Project Manager assesses the potential impact on:

- Scope and deliverables.
- Schedule and milestones.
- Cost and resource requirements.
- Quality and acceptance criteria.
- Legal and regulatory obligations.
- Information security and privacy.
- Project risks and dependencies.
- Business operations and continuity.

**Step 3 — Escalation Brief**

For matters requiring governance intervention, the Project Manager prepares a concise Escalation Brief containing:

- Issue description and known background.
- Date identified and current status.
- Root cause or current understanding of the cause.
- Affected deliverables and project baseline or planning assumption.
- Potential schedule, cost, and risk impact.
- Available response options, with advantages and disadvantages.
- Recommended action.
- Requested decision and required response date.

Urgent protective action and incident reporting must not be delayed while a written brief is prepared.

**Step 4 — Decision and Documentation**

The authorized decision-maker records the decision and any required conditions.

The Project Manager updates the relevant project registers, action records, and baselines where formally approved.

For ordinary governance matters, a response deadline should be agreed according to impact and urgency, normally within one or two business days where practicable.

Critical incidents must follow immediate escalation and applicable legal response procedures.

### 5.4 Approval and Decision Traceability

The following decision boundaries apply:

- The Project Manager may coordinate ordinary operational decisions within delegated authority.
- Material scope, schedule, and budget baseline changes require the applicable formal approval.
- Formal commercial commitments must be approved or executed by the authorized company signatory.
- Legal advisers provide professional advice but do not automatically hold company approval authority.
- Verbal Sponsor endorsement does not constitute formal approval of project baselines.
- Material approvals must be recorded in the controlled company document repository.

## 6. Security, Data Privacy, and Information Handling

### 6.1 Information Classification

Project information must be handled according to its confidentiality and business sensitivity.

| Classification | Examples | Handling Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Approved website content, published service catalogue, sanitized public project documentation | May be distributed through approved public channels. |
| Internal | General project updates, non-sensitive task records, internal procedures | Restricted to authorized project participants and approved systems. |
| Confidential | Commercial proposals, client information, contractual documents, financial details, non-public business information | Access restricted to authorized personnel; approved business channels only. |
| Restricted / Sensitive | Credentials, private keys, sensitive personal data, security incident details, privileged legal material | Secure storage and strictly controlled access; never publish in public repositories or transmit through unauthorized channels. |

Where a document contains information belonging to more than one classification, the more restrictive handling requirement applies.

### 6.2 Credential and Secret Management

The following controls apply:

- Passwords, API keys, AWS credentials, SSH private keys, database credentials, and access tokens must never be committed to GitHub.
- Credentials must be maintained in approved secure secret storage or an appropriate password manager.
- Email and messaging channels must not be used to transmit passwords, private keys, or access tokens.
- Access must follow least-privilege principles and be restricted to authorized personnel.
- MFA must be enabled for supported privileged and administrative accounts.
- Contractor and contributor access must be reviewed and revoked when no longer required.
- Secret detection and preventive repository controls should be enabled where supported.
- Suspected credential exposure must be reported immediately and addressed through the security incident procedure.
- Public project documents must be checked for accidental disclosure before publication.

### 6.3 Personal Data Protection

Project communications and client outreach must comply with applicable Omani personal data protection requirements, including:

- Royal Decree 6/2022 — Personal Data Protection Law.
- Royal Decree 68/2026 — amendments to certain provisions of the Personal Data Protection Law.
- Ministerial Decision 34/2024 — Executive Regulation of the Personal Data Protection Law, to the extent applicable following subsequent amendments.

The following controls apply:

- Personal data must be collected and processed for defined and legitimate business purposes.
- Applicable legal conditions for processing must be identified before processing begins.
- Consent must be obtained and documented where required by law.
- Commercial marketing communications must follow applicable consent and withdrawal requirements.
- Personal data must be accessed only by authorized personnel and limited to business necessity.
- Personal data must not be stored in public GitHub repositories.
- Appropriate technical and organizational security controls must be implemented according to the nature and sensitivity of the information.
- Retention and deletion must follow applicable legal obligations and approved organizational procedures.
- Cloud providers and other third-party processors must be assessed before they are permitted to process relevant personal data.
- Cross-border transfers must be assessed against applicable legal and authorization requirements.
- Bridge of Tomorrow's obligations as a controller or processor, where applicable, must be assessed.
- Where Bridge of Tomorrow is subject to the applicable controller obligations, the company must arrange the required DPO designation in accordance with the Law and Executive Regulations.
- The identity, contact arrangements, and relevant responsibilities of the designated DPO must be documented in the appropriate restricted company records.

The DPO and qualified legal adviser must be involved in the assessment of relevant personal data processing and compliance matters where their responsibilities apply.

The appointment of a DPO does not transfer the controller's legal accountability to that individual.

The applicability of specific requirements must be confirmed against the company's actual activities and current legal framework.

### 6.4 Commercial Marketing and Client Outreach

Before dispatching commercial marketing communications involving personal data:

- Determine the applicable legal requirements for the intended outreach.
- Identify an appropriate legal basis for processing and communication.
- Obtain and retain consent where required by Omani law.
- Record relevant consent evidence and its source.
- Provide an appropriate means for recipients to withdraw consent or opt out where applicable.
- Stop relevant marketing communications promptly when a valid withdrawal is received.
- Restrict access to prospect and client contact records.
- Retain only personal information required for the approved business purpose.
- Ensure that proposals use approved service descriptions and commercial terms.
- Do not use personal contact information obtained from an unverified source without assessing whether its use is permitted.

Formal proposals must use approved commercial templates and must not make commitments outside the company's authorized capabilities.

### 6.5 Data Breach and Security Incident Notification

Any suspected personal data breach or significant cybersecurity incident must be reported internally immediately through the approved incident channel.

The initial response must include:

1. Reporting the incident to the designated Security Owner, once appointed, and the Project Manager through the approved incident channel.
2. Informing the DPO where appointed and the authorized company representative as appropriate.
3. Taking reasonable steps to contain the incident and protect affected systems and information.
4. Preserving relevant evidence and documenting known facts.
5. Assessing the incident's scope, impact, affected data, and applicable notification obligations.
6. Coordinating required external communications through authorized personnel.

Under the currently published MTCIT guidance on Oman's Personal Data Protection Law and Executive Regulation:

- The controller must notify the competent department of the Ministry within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach where the breach threatens the rights of affected data subjects.
- The data subject must also be notified within 72 hours where the breach causes or is likely to cause serious harm or high risk, subject to the applicable legal requirements.

The DPO and qualified legal adviser must assess whether the relevant notification thresholds have been met and coordinate required notifications.

Internal escalation must not delay any applicable statutory notification deadline.

Incident details must be retained in a restricted Incident Register and must not be circulated through general project communication channels.

The applicable legal requirements and reporting procedures must be verified against the current law and regulations at the time of an incident.

### 6.6 Public and External Communications

No public announcement, press release, LinkedIn publication, or external statement concerning confidential Project Genesis activities, internal decisions, or client engagements may be made without the required written approval from the authorized company representative.

After formal designation, the Project Sponsor may exercise the relevant communication approval authority within the applicable company delegation.

Approved public communications must not disclose:

- Confidential company information.
- Personal or client data.
- Private contractual or legal details.
- Sensitive Commercial Registration information.
- Credentials or security-sensitive details.
- Unverified service claims or unauthorized commercial commitments.

Public documentation of Project Genesis as a professional work record must remain consistent with company confidentiality and information security requirements.

## 7. Meeting Records, Reporting, and Performance Monitoring

### 7.1 Weekly Status Reporting

The Project Manager will prepare a weekly report containing:

- Overall project RAG status.
- Progress against deliverables D1–D7.
- Milestone progress and forecast completion.
- Schedule position against the approved baseline, once established.
- Budget position against the approved cost baseline, once established.
- Significant risks and issues.
- Outstanding decisions and approvals.
- Actions due and responsible owners.
- Dependencies and resource constraints.
- Priorities for the next reporting period.

The report must distinguish between:

- Detailed cost forecast.
- Formally approved Cost Baseline, once established.
- Planning schedule assumptions.
- Formally approved Schedule Baseline, once established.

The report must not present provisional estimates as approved project commitments.

### 7.2 Communication Effectiveness

The Project Manager will review communication effectiveness throughout the project.

Relevant indicators may include:

- Timeliness of required project updates.
- Percentage of material decisions documented.
- Number of overdue communication-related action items.
- Time taken to escalate significant blockers.
- Completion of required approvals before dependent work proceeds.
- Availability of supporting records for milestone acceptance.
- Timeliness of stakeholder feedback at designated review points.
- Closure of communication-related issues.

Communication effectiveness is assessed by the quality, timeliness, accuracy, and traceability of information rather than the volume of meetings or messages.

### 7.3 Continuous Improvement

Communication practices may be adjusted based on:

- Stakeholder feedback.
- Actual project complexity.
- Resource availability.
- Changes in stakeholder responsibilities.
- Delivery progress and dependencies.
- Lessons learned from coordination and escalation activities.

Material changes to communication responsibilities, decision authority, security requirements, or approved communication arrangements must be documented and reviewed through the appropriate governance process.
