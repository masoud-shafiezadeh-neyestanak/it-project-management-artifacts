# IT Project Management Artifacts & Governance Framework

### Project Genesis — Enterprise Operational Infrastructure & Service Workflows Launch

Welcome to the central repository for practical IT project management artifacts, governance documentation, and operational workflows.

This repository documents the planning and development of Project Genesis, an operational readiness initiative for an IT and business services enterprise in the Sultanate of Oman.

It demonstrates the practical application of project management concepts and tools informed by the **Google Project Management Professional Certificate (Coursera)**, adapted to the project's business, technical, and operational requirements.

Project Genesis follows a **Hybrid Project Management approach**, combining structured governance and documented approval controls with iterative delivery practices where appropriate.

The purpose of this repository is to show how project management concepts can be translated into practical documentation, defined responsibilities, measurable deliverables, and traceable project controls.

---

## Project Overview

Project Genesis aims to establish the foundational digital infrastructure, corporate presence, commercial documentation, and operational workflows required to support an IT and business services enterprise in Oman.

### Key Project Objectives

- Establish secure corporate digital infrastructure and appropriate cloud-based operational services.
- Develop and launch a bilingual corporate website in Arabic and English.
- Prepare standardized commercial and legal document templates.
- Establish centralized operational and project management workflows using Asana.
- Develop a structured digital service catalogue.
- Initiate business development through five formal proposals to qualified SME prospects.
- Establish documented governance, risk management, communication, and project control practices.
- Complete project handover and maintain records of deliverable acceptance and closure decisions.

### Project Parameters

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Planned Duration | 8 calendar weeks, nominally 40 business days based on a five-day working week |
| Project Location | Bousher and Barka, Sultanate of Oman |
| Initial Budget Estimate | USD 1,500–2,500 (approximately OMR 570–960) |
| Budget Status | Preliminary estimate — subject to detailed estimation and Sponsor approval |
| Delivery Approach | Hybrid Project Management |
| Primary Coordination Tools | Asana, GitHub, and Google Workspace |

The planned duration and initial budget estimate are planning assumptions, not approved baselines. Project start and completion dates, detailed cost estimates, and formal baseline approvals remain subject to confirmation.

The project scope, schedule, budget, risks, and approval status are maintained in their respective project documents.

## Repository Structure & Artifacts

This section distinguishes documents currently published in the repository from artifacts planned for subsequent project phases.

### 01. Initiation — `/01-initiation`

Establishing the project foundation, governance structure, stakeholder responsibilities, and initial authorization.

**Currently published documents:**

- [Project Charter (GEN-CH-001)](01-initiation/01-project-charter.md) — Defines the project objectives, business case, high-level scope, assumptions, constraints, governance structure, and authorization requirements.
- [Stakeholder Register & RACI Matrix (GEN-STK-001)](01-initiation/02-stakeholder-and-raci-register.md) — Identifies project stakeholders, evaluates their influence and interest, documents expectations and engagement strategies, and defines responsibility assignments.

### 02. Planning & Governance — `/02-planning`

Establishing the scope, schedule, cost, risk, communication, and quality management arrangements required for controlled project delivery.

**Currently published documents:**

- [Scope Baseline (currently GEN-WBS-001)](02-planning/01-scope-baseline.md) — Integrates the Project Scope Statement, Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), WBS Dictionary, deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and constraints.
- [Risk Register (GEN-RSK-001)](02-planning/02-risk-register.md) — Identifies and assesses project risks using a qualitative Probability–Impact (P×I) assessment and documents risk responses, ownership, monitoring, and escalation.
- [Communication Management Plan (GEN-COM-001)](02-planning/03-communication-plan.md) — Defines stakeholder communication requirements, channels, reporting arrangements, meeting practices, escalation procedures, and information security considerations.

**Additional planning documents under development:**

- Schedule Baseline (GEN-SCH-001) — Defines activity sequencing, duration estimates, milestones, dependencies, and resource considerations. Critical-path validation remains subject to schedule review.
- Budget Baseline (GEN-BGT-001) — Establishes cost categories, estimation assumptions, contingency considerations, budget monitoring, and expenditure controls.
- Quality Management Plan (GEN-QMP-001) — Defines quality requirements, verification arrangements, deliverable acceptance criteria, and quality control practices.
- Project Kickoff Agenda (GEN-KOF-001) — Establishes the proposed kickoff agenda, stakeholder alignment, communication expectations, and execution readiness requirements.

### 03. Execution — `/03-execution` *(Planned)*

Implementing the approved project scope through coordinated technical activities, operational workflows, and deliverable development.

Planned work areas include:

- **Hybrid Delivery & Operational Workflows:** Task coordination, iterative delivery, backlog management, workflow configuration, and execution tracking using Asana and GitHub.
- **Technical & Operational Deliverables:** Digital infrastructure, corporate email, cloud services, bilingual website implementation, and operational system configuration.
- **Commercial & Legal Template Suite:** Preparation and review of standardized business and contractual templates.
- **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Documented administrative processes, IT support workflows, service delivery procedures, and implementation checklists.

Planned commercial and legal templates:

| Deliverable | Document ID |
|---|---|
| Statement of Work (SOW) | GEN-SOW-001 |
| Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) | GEN-NDA-001 |
| Master Services Agreement (MSA) | GEN-MSA-001 |
| Service Level Agreement (SLA) | GEN-SLA-001 |
| Client Commercial Proposal | GEN-PRP-001 |

**Target location for commercial and legal templates:**  
`03-execution/deliverables/commercial-legal-templates/`

These templates are intended to be reviewed by appropriately qualified advisers before organizational or client-facing use.

### 04. Monitoring & Controlling — `/04-monitoring-control` *(Planned)*

Monitoring project performance, evaluating deviations, managing changes, and maintaining control over approved project baselines.

Planned artifacts include:

- **Change Log:** Tracking proposed, approved, and rejected change requests, including impact assessments and approval records.
- **Issue Log:** Recording project issues, ownership, escalation, monitoring, and resolution.
- **Decision Log:** Maintaining traceable records of material technical, commercial, financial, and governance decisions.
- **Deliverable Acceptance Register:** Recording reviews, acceptance decisions, outstanding items, and approved exceptions for deliverables D1–D7.
- **Status Reports:** Reporting project progress, milestone status, schedule variance, budget performance, key risks, and decisions required.

### 05. Closure — `/05-closure` *(Planned)*

Formally closing the project, transferring operational assets, documenting lessons learned, and evaluating delivery performance.

Planned artifacts include:

- **Project Closeout Report:** Evaluating project performance against approved scope, schedule, cost, and acceptance requirements.
- **Asset Handover Checklist:** Verifying the transfer and ownership of cloud accounts, technical documentation, repositories, operational resources, and relevant business assets.
- **Lessons Learned Register:** Recording retrospective findings, improvement opportunities, and recommendations for future projects.
- **Final Budget Summary:** Reconciling the approved budget, commitments, actual costs, and outstanding financial obligations.

### Repository Information Security

This repository follows an information security and controlled-documentation approach.

- Passwords, API keys, private keys, access tokens, and other secrets must not be committed to GitHub.
- Public repositories must not contain confidential business documents or client personal data.
- Sensitive legal, financial, and commercial documents must be maintained in appropriately restricted storage.
- Access to cloud services and project tools must follow authorized access and least-privilege principles.
- Material project decisions, approvals, and changes must be documented through designated project records.

### Project Management Approach

**Hybrid Project Management**

Project Genesis combines:

- Structured project governance and documented approval controls.
- Predictive planning for scope, milestones, budget, and governance.
- Iterative and Agile delivery practices where appropriate.
- Continuous risk monitoring and stakeholder communication.
- Documented deliverable verification, acceptance, and project closure.

The approach is tailored to the project's scale, operational requirements, dependencies, and available resources.

## Project Portfolio Objective

Project Genesis is intended to demonstrate how project management concepts can be translated into a structured and practical operational initiative.

Rather than presenting isolated project management templates, the repository connects governance documents, planning arrangements, execution workflows, monitoring controls, and closure artifacts into an integrated project environment.

The objective is to establish a traceable project lifecycle in which every major deliverable has:

- A defined scope and expected outcome.
- An assigned owner and accountability structure.
- Appropriate schedule and resource considerations.
- Identified risks and response actions.
- Documented acceptance criteria.
- Traceable progress, decisions, and approval records.
