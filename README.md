# IT Project Management Artifacts & Governance Framework 
### Project Genesis — Operational Infrastructure & Service Workflows

Welcome to the central repository for standardized IT project management frameworks and artifacts. This project (**Project Genesis**) demonstrates end-to-end IT operations governance, chartering, and risk management built upon PMI standards and the Google Project Management framework.

---

##  Repository Structure & Artifacts

###  01. Initiation Phase (`/01-initiation`)
- **Project Charter:** Defining high-level objectives, business cases, scope, constraints, and authority levels.
- **Stakeholder Register:** Identification, power/interest mapping, and communication management.

###  02. Planning & Governance (`/02-planning`)
- **RACI Matrix:** Clear allocation of Roles and Responsibilities across IT workflows.
- **Risk Register:** Comprehensive risk identification, probability/impact rating, and mitigation strategies.
- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** Scope decomposition into actionable deliverables.

###  03. Execution & Agile Workflows (`/03-execution-agile`)
- **Agile Task Framework:** Task structure and sprint organization applied in tools like Asana and Google Tasks.

###  04. Closure & Retrospective (`/04-closure`)
- **Project Closeout Report:** Deliverable acceptance, handoff criteria, and lessons learned.
