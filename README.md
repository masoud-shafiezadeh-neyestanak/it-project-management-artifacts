# IT Project Management Artifacts & Governance Framework

### Project Genesis — Enterprise Operational Infrastructure & Service Workflows Launch

This repository contains the project management and operational documentation for **Project Genesis**, a real business initiative being implemented within a company registered in the Sultanate of Oman with an active Commercial Registration (CR).

Project Genesis is not a simulated case study or a demonstration-only exercise. It is an actual company initiative focused on establishing and strengthening the organization's digital infrastructure, corporate presence, commercial documentation, internal workflows, and operational readiness.

The project is managed using a **Hybrid Project Management approach**, combining structured governance and documented approval controls with iterative delivery practices where appropriate. The management methods are informed by the **Google Project Management Professional Certificate (Coursera)** and are adapted to the company's actual business context, objectives, available resources, and operating requirements.

This repository serves two connected purposes:

- **Operational use:** supporting the planning, coordination, implementation, monitoring, and closure of Project Genesis.
- **Professional record:** maintaining a traceable record of project management work and practical experience gained by applying learned concepts in a real organizational environment.

The public repository is maintained with due consideration for business confidentiality, information security, and applicable legal requirements. Sensitive company records, CR identifiers, credentials, client information, and confidential commercial or legal documents are not intended for public disclosure.

## Project Overview

Project Genesis aims to establish the foundational capabilities required to support the company's IT and business services activities in Oman. The initiative brings together digital infrastructure, a corporate web presence, standardized business documentation, operational workflows, a digital service catalogue, and initial client outreach preparation.

### Key Project Objectives

- Establish secure corporate digital infrastructure and appropriate cloud-based operational services.
- Develop and launch a bilingual corporate website in Arabic and English.
- Prepare standardized commercial and legal document templates for appropriate professional review.
- Establish centralized operational and project management workflows using Asana.
- Develop a structured digital service catalogue aligned with the company's verified and authorized business activities.
- Prepare and dispatch five formal proposals to qualified SME prospects, with dispatch and follow-up records.
- Apply documented governance, risk management, communication, change-control, and project monitoring practices.
- Complete deliverable reviews, operational handover, and project closure documentation.

## Repository Structure & Project Documentation

The repository distinguishes between documents currently published and documents planned or being prepared for later project phases. A document being described below does not by itself mean that it has been formally approved or that the associated project work has been completed.

### 01. Initiation — `/01-initiation`

Establishes the project foundation, governance context, stakeholder responsibilities, and authorization arrangements.

**Currently published:**

- [Project Charter — GEN-CH-001](01-initiation/01-project-charter.md): Defines the project purpose, business case, objectives, high-level scope, assumptions, constraints, governance structure, and authorization requirements.
- [Stakeholder Register & RACI Matrix — GEN-STK-001](01-initiation/02-stakeholder-and-raci-register.md): Identifies project stakeholders, considers their influence and interest, records expectations and engagement approaches, and assigns responsibilities and accountability.

### 02. Planning & Governance — `/02-planning`

Establishes the scope, schedule, cost, risk, communication, and quality arrangements needed to plan and control delivery.

**Currently published:**

- [Scope Baseline — currently identified as GEN-WBS-001](02-planning/01-scope-baseline.md): Integrates the scope statement, Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), WBS Dictionary, deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions, constraints, and scope control. The document identifier is flagged for harmonization with the `GEN-SBL-001` identifier used in related planning references.
- [Risk Register — GEN-RSK-001](02-planning/02-risk-register.md): Identifies and assesses project risks using a qualitative Probability–Impact (P×I) method and records risk responses, ownership, monitoring, and escalation arrangements.
- [Communication Management Plan — GEN-COM-001](02-planning/03-communication-plan.md): Defines stakeholder communication requirements, channels, reporting arrangements, meeting practices, escalation procedures, and information-handling considerations.

**Planning documents prepared or planned for publication:**

- **Schedule Baseline — GEN-SCH-001:** A draft has been prepared for review. It describes activity sequencing, estimated durations, dependencies, milestones, and resource considerations. Dates, working-calendar assumptions, and schedule logic require validation before approval.
- **Budget Baseline — GEN-BGT-001:** A draft has been prepared for review. It establishes cost categories, estimation assumptions, recurring-cost considerations, contingency treatment, budget monitoring, and expenditure controls. Detailed cost estimates and Sponsor approval remain outstanding.
- **Quality Management Plan — GEN-QMP-001:** Planned; will define quality requirements, verification arrangements, deliverable acceptance criteria, and quality-control practices.
- **Project Kickoff Agenda — GEN-KOF-001:** Planned; will define the kickoff agenda, stakeholder alignment, communication expectations, responsibilities, and execution-readiness topics.

### 03. Execution — `/03-execution` *(Planned repository phase)*

Supports implementation of the project scope through coordinated technical activities, operational workflows, and development of project deliverables.

Planned work areas include:

- **Hybrid Delivery & Operational Workflows:** Task coordination, iterative delivery, backlog management, workflow configuration, and execution tracking using Asana and GitHub.
- **Technical & Operational Deliverables:** Digital infrastructure, corporate email, cloud services, bilingual website implementation, and operational system configuration.
- **Commercial & Legal Template Suite:** Preparation and review of reusable business and contractual templates.
- **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Documented administrative processes, IT support workflows, service delivery procedures, and implementation checklists.

Planned commercial and legal templates:

| Deliverable | Document ID |
|---|---|
| Statement of Work (SOW) | GEN-SOW-001 |
| Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) | GEN-NDA-001 |
| Master Services Agreement (MSA) | GEN-MSA-001 |
| Service Level Agreement (SLA) | GEN-SLA-001 |
| Client Commercial Proposal | GEN-PRP-001 |

**Target location:** `03-execution/deliverables/commercial-legal-templates/`

These templates are intended for organizational use only after appropriate review and authorization by qualified advisers and designated company representatives.

### 04. Monitoring & Controlling — `/04-monitoring-control` *(Planned repository phase)*

Supports monitoring of project performance, evaluation of deviations, change control, issue resolution, and maintenance of traceable project records.

Planned artifacts include:

- **Change Log:** Proposed, approved, and rejected changes, including impact assessments and authorization records.
- **Issue Log:** Project issues, ownership, escalation, monitoring, and resolution.
- **Decision Log:** Traceable records of material technical, commercial, financial, and governance decisions.
- **Deliverable Acceptance Register:** Reviews, acceptance decisions, outstanding items, and approved exceptions for deliverables D1–D7.
- **Status Reports:** Progress, milestone status, schedule variance, budget position, key risks, and decisions required.

### 05. Closure — `/05-closure` *(Planned repository phase)*

Supports formal project closure, asset handover, lessons learned, and evaluation of delivery performance.

Planned artifacts include:

- **Project Closeout Report:** Evaluation of project performance against approved scope, schedule, cost, and acceptance requirements.
- **Asset Handover Checklist:** Verification and transfer of cloud accounts, technical documentation, repositories, operational resources, and relevant business assets under authorized organizational control.
- **Lessons Learned Register:** Retrospective findings, improvement opportunities, and recommendations for future work.
- **Final Budget Summary:** Reconciliation of approved budget, commitments, actual expenditure, and outstanding financial obligations.
