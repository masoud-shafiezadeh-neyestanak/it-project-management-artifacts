# IT Project Management Artifacts & Governance Framework

### Project Genesis — Enterprise Operational Infrastructure & Service Workflows Launch

This repository contains the project management and operational documentation for **Project Genesis**, a real business initiative being implemented within **Bridge of Tomorrow**, a company registered in the Sultanate of Oman with an active Commercial Registration (CR).

Project Genesis is not a simulated case study or a demonstration-only exercise. It is an actual company initiative focused on establishing and strengthening the organization's digital infrastructure, corporate presence, commercial documentation, internal workflows, and operational readiness.

The project follows a **Hybrid Project Management approach**, combining structured governance and documented approval controls with iterative delivery practices where appropriate. The management methods are informed by the **Google Project Management Professional Certificate (Coursera)** and adapted to the company's actual business context, objectives, available resources, and operating requirements.

This repository serves two connected purposes:

- **Operational use:** Supporting the planning, coordination, implementation, monitoring, and closure of Project Genesis.
- **Professional record:** Maintaining a traceable record of project management work and practical experience gained by applying project management concepts in a real organizational environment.

The public repository is maintained with due consideration for business confidentiality, information security, and applicable legal requirements. Sensitive company records, CR identifiers, credentials, client information, and confidential commercial or legal documents are not intended for public disclosure.

## Project Overview

Project Genesis aims to establish the foundational capabilities required to support Bridge of Tomorrow's IT and business services activities in Oman.

The initiative brings together digital infrastructure, a corporate web presence, standardized business documentation, operational workflows, a digital service catalogue, and initial client outreach preparation.

### Key Project Objectives

- Establish secure corporate digital infrastructure and appropriate cloud-based operational services.
- Develop and launch a bilingual corporate website in Arabic and English.
- Prepare standardized commercial and legal document templates for appropriate professional review.
- Establish centralized operational and project management workflows using Asana.
- Develop a structured digital service catalogue aligned with the company's verified and authorized business activities.
- Prepare and dispatch five formal proposals to qualified SME prospects, with dispatch and follow-up records.
- Apply documented governance, risk management, communication, change-control, and quality management practices.
- Complete deliverable reviews, operational handover, and project closure documentation.

## Repository Structure & Project Documentation

The repository is organized around the major project management phases. The documentation distinguishes between published planning artifacts, documents prepared for later phases, and work that has actually been implemented or accepted.

Publication of a document does not by itself mean that the document has been formally approved or that the corresponding project work has been completed.

### 01. Initiation — `/01-initiation`

Establishes the project foundation, governance context, stakeholder responsibilities, and authorization arrangements.

**Published documents:**

- [Project Charter — GEN-CH-001](01-initiation/01-project-charter.md) — Defines the project purpose, business case, objectives, high-level scope, assumptions, constraints, governance structure, and authorization requirements.
- [Stakeholder Register & RACI Matrix — GEN-STK-001](01-initiation/02-stakeholder-and-raci-register.md) — Identifies project stakeholders, records their interests and expectations, and defines responsibilities and accountability across project work packages.

### 02. Planning & Governance — `/02-planning`

Establishes the scope, schedule, cost, risk, communication, and quality arrangements needed to plan and control project delivery.

**Published documents:**

- [Scope Baseline — GEN-SBL-001](02-planning/01-scope-baseline.md) — Defines the scope statement, Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), WBS Dictionary, deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions, constraints, and scope control arrangements.
- [Risk Register — GEN-RSK-001](02-planning/02-risk-register.md) — Identifies and assesses project risks using a qualitative Probability–Impact (P×I) method and records risk responses, ownership, monitoring, and escalation arrangements.
- [Communication Management Plan — GEN-COM-001](02-planning/03-communication-plan.md) — Defines stakeholder communication requirements, channels, reporting arrangements, meeting practices, escalation procedures, and information-handling considerations.
- [Schedule Baseline — GEN-SCH-001](02-planning/04-schedule-baseline.md) — Documents the planned activity sequence, estimated durations, dependencies, milestones, scheduling assumptions, and validation requirements.
- [Budget Baseline — GEN-BGT-001](02-planning/05-budget-baseline.md) — Documents cost categories, preliminary estimates, estimation assumptions, recurring-cost considerations, and budget monitoring and control arrangements.
- [Quality Management Plan — GEN-QMP-001](02-planning/06-quality-management-plan.md) — Defines quality objectives, quality assurance and control practices, deliverable-specific criteria, verification arrangements, and acceptance procedures.

**Planned planning and governance document:**

- **Project Kickoff Agenda — GEN-KOF-001:** To support stakeholder alignment, communication expectations, responsibility confirmation, and execution-readiness discussions.

**Planning and approval note:**

The planning documents are published as working project records. Their publication does not establish formal Sponsor approval of the project charter, scope, schedule, budget, or other management plans. Formal authorization and baseline approval must be evidenced through the designated project approval records.

## Project Governance & Responsibilities

Project Genesis uses a defined governance structure to distinguish project coordination, delivery responsibilities, review activities, and formal authorization.

- **Project Sponsor / Authorized Company Representative:** Provides strategic direction and project-level decisions once formally designated.
- **Project Manager:** Coordinates planning, delivery oversight, stakeholder communication, risk management, documentation, monitoring, and escalation.
- **Technical Contributor:** Supports technical design, implementation, testing, and technical documentation when formally assigned.
- **Marketing Contributor:** Supports website content, service catalogue development, and client outreach when formally assigned.
- **External Legal Adviser:** Provides qualified legal review of relevant commercial and contractual documents when engaged.
- **Authorized Company Signatory:** Executes documents and commitments within formally established authority.
- **Privacy / Data Protection Specialist:** Provides specialist privacy advice when required and appropriately appointed.
- **Other Specialist Contributors:** May be engaged where specific technical, operational, or regulatory expertise is needed.

Role descriptions and RACI assignments do not, by themselves, establish that a person has been appointed, contracted, or granted legal or financial authority.

The Project Manager coordinates the work and governance process but does not replace qualified technical, legal, privacy, or other specialist advisers.
